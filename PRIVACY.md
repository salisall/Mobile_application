# Privacy Policy: Manifest

_Last updated: November 22, 2026_

Manifest ("the app") is a personal manifestation journal. Your privacy matters, so the app is built to keep everything on your device.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** The app has no account and no sign-up. It doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Where your data is stored
Your intentions, check-ins, signs and gratitude entries are saved **only on your device**, in the app's local storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

## Deleting your data
To delete individual items, use the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
