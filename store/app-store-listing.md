**Description:**
Manifest helps you turn your intentions into a daily practice.

• Set intentions in the present tense, for love, money, career, health, home, growth or travel
• Check in each day after you visualize, and build streaks
• Get a fresh affirmation every morning
• Log signs and synchronicities as evidence it's on its way
• Keep a gratitude journal: 3 things a day
• See your progress: check-ins this week, best streaks, and everything you've manifested
• Light and dark mode

Private by design: no account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

**Review notes for Apple:**
